French Toast

Ingredients

  • 8 slices of bread
  • 1/4 cup all purpose flour
  • 2/3 of any milk
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/4 cup of granulated sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Equipment

  • Skillet pan
  • Spatula
  • Blender
  • Large bowl

Directions

  1. Cook the skillet in medium heat.
  2. Blend the eggs milk flour sugar salt cinnamon and vanilla together until smooth.
  3. After blending mix the flour with the blended ingredients, if you prefer whisking then whisk until its combined.
  4. Dip the slices of bread into the batter making sure both sides are coated, then set on the skillet.
  5. Cook unitl both sides are golden brown. once done set them aside on a plate to be served with syrup or coated sugar for breakfast.
